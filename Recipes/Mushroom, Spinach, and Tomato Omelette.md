## Mushroom, Spinach, and Tomato Omelette

Ingredients (Updated)

2 large eggs

1/2 cup sliced mushrooms

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 tablespoon olive oil

1/2 cup fresh spinach

1/2 cup sliced tomatoes

1/4 cup shredded cheese (your choice)

1/2 teaspoon paprika

## Instructions

1. Sauté the Veggies: Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sliced mushrooms, and sauté until the mushrooms are golden and tender.


2. Add Spinach and Tomatoes: Stir in the fresh spinach and cook until wilted. Add the sliced tomatoes and cook for another minute.


3. Prepare the Eggs: In a bowl, whisk the eggs until well beaten. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.


4. Cook the Omelette: Pour the beaten eggs over the sautéed veggies in the skillet. Let it cook on medium-low heat until the edges start to set.


5. Add Cheese: Sprinkle the shredded cheese on top of the eggs. Cover the skillet and let it cook until the cheese melts and the eggs are fully set.


6. Serve: Gently fold the omelette in half and serve warm.



## Enjoy your flavorful omelette!
